Built for reliability.

Every component is designed to fail safely and recover without losing a lead.

Idempotent submissions

Repeat the same request safely. Duplicate leads are caught at the queue boundary, not in the database.

Backpressure-safe

Streams cap at ~10k entries. Old entries auto-trim; new entries always find a slot.

Observable by default

Structured logs, idempotency-failure counters, and per-stage error visibility. No black boxes.
